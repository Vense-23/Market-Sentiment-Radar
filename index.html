  • CRWV (CoreWeave)
    CoreWeave beats on revenue and lifts guidance, easing AI slowdown concerns
    翻译：CoreWeave营收超预期并上调业绩指引，缓解了人们对AI放缓的担忧。
    Here are some numbers I got from CRWV's Q3 earning. CRWV's debt went from $51.2B in Q2 to $66.2B in Q3, a 28% QoQ jump. CRWV's interest payment jumped from 640 million to 846 million from Q2 to Q3. That is a 32% QoQ increase. Q3's income has a massive beat, caused by switching to higher priced short term 6 month long contracts and having a one time price increase. This results in a 25% price increase on average for Q3 compared to Q2. Keep in mind that bond yield is only going up. Fed is hiking the rate in December. CRWV's debt growth is constant. Can they increase the price by 25% every quarter? Can they sign more expensive 6-day contracts in the future instead of their more pricy 6-month contracts in last quarter?
    翻译：这是我从CoreWeave第三季度财报中获得的一些数据。CoreWeave的债务从第二季度的512亿美元增至第三季度的662亿美元，环比增长28%。CoreWeave的利息支付从第二季度的6.4亿美元增至第三季度的8.46亿美元，环比增长32%。第三季度收入大幅超出预期，原因在于转向了价格更高的短期6个月合同，并进行了一次性价格上涨。这导致第三季度平均价格比第二季度上涨了25%。请记住，债券收益率只会上升。美联储将在12月加息。CoreWeave的债务增长是持续的。他们能否每季度将价格提高25%？他们未来能否签署更昂贵的6天合同，而不是上季度价格较高的6个月合同？
