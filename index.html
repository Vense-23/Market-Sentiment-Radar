  • AI will prevail, most AI companies will go bankrupt. This is almost a carbon copy of Dot Com. Dot Com was fueled by falling rate from Jan 1995 to Dec 1998. Credit was essentially free as people expected the rate to continue downwards. Demand for internet infrastructure was infinite as companies build internet for the sake of building internet. Things changed in 1999 as the 10y yield roared from 4.6% to 6.7% in one year. Internet firms that were losing money suddenly could not sell their shares or issue bonds under 10%. Suddenly building internet only made sense when real consumers would use it. Mass bankruptcy occured about 1 year later. The same thing is happening to AI right now. META had to sell their shares for $625 when the market price was $700 to investers. ORCL had to offer $116 when the stock was $130 from public market. PC investors demand >10% yield on their bonds.
    翻译：AI会胜出，但大多数AI公司会破产。这几乎是互联网泡沫的翻版。互联网泡沫是由1995年1月至1998年12月利率下降推动的。信贷几乎是免费的，因为人们预期利率会继续下降。对互联网基础设施的需求是无限的，因为公司为了建设互联网而建设。1999年情况发生了变化，10年期国债收益率在一年内从4.6%飙升至6.7%。亏损的互联网公司突然无法以低于10%的利率出售股票或发行债券。突然之间，只有当真实消费者使用时，建设互联网才有意义。大约一年后，大规模破产发生了。同样的事情现在正发生在AI领域。META不得不以625美元的价格向投资者出售股票，当时市场价格是700美元。ORCL不得不以116美元的价格向公开市场提供股票，当时股价是130美元。PC投资者要求债券收益率超过10%。

    翻译：纳斯达克100指数进入调整，原因是大型科技公司的业绩加剧了对AI的担忧。
