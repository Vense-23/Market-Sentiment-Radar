    Kavukcuoglu, head of Google's DeepMind division, said on Wednesday that Google is set to unveil its latest flagship model, Gemini 4. This development has been highly anticipated, as the tech giant has lagged behind rivals Anthropic and OpenAI in the AI model race. Kavukcuoglu added that Gemini 4 is currently in the early stages of "post-training" development - fine-tuning the underlying AI model. He stated that he hopes the model will be released "well before" the end of this year. "Our intention is to roll out an early post‑training version as soon as possible, because we've already seen promising results and are very excited," he said.
    翻译：Google DeepMind部门负责人Kavukcuoglu周三表示，Google即将发布其最新的旗舰模型Gemini 4。这一进展备受期待，因为这家科技巨头在AI模型竞赛中一直落后于竞争对手Anthropic和OpenAI。Kavukcuoglu补充说，Gemini 4目前正处于“后期训练”开发的早期阶段——对底层AI模型进行微调。他表示，他希望该模型能在今年年底“远早于”发布。他说：“我们的目标是尽快推出早期后期训练版本，因为我们已经看到了有希望的结果并且非常兴奋。”

One finance industry observer on LinkedIn said the bank’s “biggest concern will most likely be the impact on their reputation,” adding that the key question is whether clients can still trust Morgan Stanley with “their most confidential, valuable and sensitive data.”
    翻译：彭博社援引匿名消息来源报道，摩根士丹利的一名员工不慎向客户发送了一份内部文件，详细列出了该行在亚洲正在进行或追踪的100多项投行交易。这份清单包括了目标进行IPO的公司以及投资这些业务的私募股权和养老基金。一位LinkedIn上的金融行业观察家表示，该银行“最大的担忧很可能在于其声誉受到的影响”，并补充说，关键问题是客户是否仍能信任摩根士丹利处理“他们最机密、最有价值和最敏感的数据”。
