  • When China first allowed banks to package and sell nonperforming consumer loans in 2021, just 18.6 billion yuan ($2.8 billion) was transferred off banks’ balance sheets and sold to distressed-asset managers by the end of that year. By the close of 2025, that figure had surged more than 20 times, to about 454 billion yuan, according to Ruiunion Credit Platform, a Shenzhen-based company which provides asset valuations. “This isn’t a story about reckless borrowing,” says Huang Yuying, 32, the manager of a midsize debt-collection company in the eastern city of Hefei. “More often it’s because of failed businesses, job losses or sharp drops in income.”
    翻译：中国在2021年首次允许银行打包出售不良消费贷款时，当年底仅有186亿元人民币（28亿美元）从银行资产负债表转出并出售给不良资产管理公司。根据深圳资产估值公司瑞联盟信用平台的数据，到2025年底，这一数字已飙升20多倍，达到约4540亿元人民币。“这并非一个关于鲁莽借贷的故事，”32岁的黄玉英说，她是合肥一家中型催收公司的经理。“更多时候是因为企业倒闭、失业或收入骤降。”

  • 2. CVS (CVS Health Corporation)
    $CVS- They just announced they're breaking themselves up, but their retail pharmacy business is rotten and as a stand alone stock there will be nothing, but short sellers circling the waters. Background: CVS Health Corp is breaking itself up into two separate companies after 8 years of being a combined company with a PBM, CVS Caremark, Health Insurer (Aetna), retail CVS Pharmacies, CVS Specialty, etc. The retail business will be separated to stop anti trust lawsuits, regulators and traded as it's own company. That said, anyone familiar with CVS retail stores knows they have gigantic losses from theft/shrink nationwide, few stores have loss prevention or security, and their retail stores are ghost towns with the exception of the pharmacy where prescriptions/scripts are filled.
    翻译：CVS——他们刚刚宣布将拆分自身，但其零售药店业务已经腐烂，作为独立股票，只会吸引做空者。背景：CVS健康公司在合并了药房福利管理公司CVS Caremark、健康保险公司（Aetna）、CVS零售药店、CVS Specialty等8年后，正拆分为两家独立公司。零售业务将剥离以阻止反垄断诉讼和监管，并作为一家独立公司交易。话虽如此，任何熟悉CVS零售店的人都知道，它们在全国范围内因盗窃/损耗而遭受巨大损失，很少有门店设有防损或安保人员，除了药房（用于配药）之外，其零售店都是空城。
  • 3. SMCI (Super Micro Computer, Inc.)
    In the last 2 trading sessions, SMCI seemed to have outperformed Dell by falling less and recovering more. Reasons why it is SMCI's turn to outperform vs. Dell: - It fell much more and is close to its lows - Much lower valuation - Pure play on AI with stronger growth - Turning around with new CFO, margins and profitability, better working capital management, less leveraged etc - Supports SPCX strong growth as articulated by Elon Musk over the weekend Additional reasons why Dell should underperform SMCI - Exposed to PC business, which is a challenged segment (based on Intel) - Trump pump effect over, post the midterms.
    翻译：在过去的两个交易日中，SMCI似乎跑赢了戴尔，跌幅更小，反弹更多。SMCI跑赢戴尔的原因是：——它跌得更多，接近低点；——估值低得多；——纯粹的人工智能业务，增长更强劲；——新任CFO带来转机，利润率和盈利能力提升，营运资本管理更好，杠杆率降低等；——支持Elon Musk周末阐述的SPCX强劲增长。戴尔跑输SMCI的额外原因：——受制于PC业务，这是一个受挑战的领域（基于英特尔）；——中期选举后，特朗普效应已过。
